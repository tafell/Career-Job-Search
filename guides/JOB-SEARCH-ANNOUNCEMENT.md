# Announce Your Job Search

Use the standalone Job Search Announcement skill to draft a LinkedIn announcement that asks for concrete help and offers assistance you genuinely want to provide.

## Use it in ChatGPT or Claude

1. Open a private conversation or project. Supply your job search profile and relevant career evidence.
2. Use either the [ChatGPT skill](../chatgpt/skills/job-search-announcement/SKILL.md) or the [Claude skill](../skills/job-search-announcement/SKILL.md). Their instructions are identical. Paste the instructions into the conversation or project; no other skill or connector is required.
3. State which search details may be public. You do not need to disclose why you are searching, your compensation, or a public email address.
4. Choose a specific way you would like to help others. Sharing opportunities, exchanging expertise, or making appropriate introductions are examples, not required commitments.
5. Use the request below. Review the draft and publish it yourself.

## Copyable request

1. "Use the standalone Job Search Announcement skill. Draft a LinkedIn post explaining what I am looking for, the value I can contribute, and specific ways my network can help. Include an offer of assistance that I choose. Use my profile and supplied career evidence. Ask for missing information and public-disclosure choices before drafting dependent claims. Give me one concise draft. Do not publish anything."

## What to expect

1. One concise draft by default. Request a more personal alternative when you want a second version.
2. Clear requests for relevant openings, introductions, or conversations rather than an unspecific appeal for help.
3. A reciprocal offer grounded in your willingness, competence, and available time.
4. An approved contact method and a separate note identifying anything you need to confirm.
5. An optional follow-up post when requested, without invented progress or automatic scheduling.

## Keep private

1. Do not commit your filled-in profile, resume, employment circumstances, or personal drafts to the public repository.
2. Do not upload private documents into a publicly shared assistant's knowledge.
3. The reusable instructions can be shared. Your personal inputs and generated posts remain yours to review and disclose.
