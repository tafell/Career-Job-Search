# Your first job search announcement

Use this guide to draft a short post telling people what work you are looking for, how they can help, and what help you can offer in return. You need a browser and access to ChatGPT or Claude. You can complete the first exercise from this page alone.

## Draft your first post

1. **Open a new conversation.** Keep this guide open. In another browser tab, open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) or [Claude](https://claude.ai/). Sign in to the assistant you choose and start a conversation that only you use.
2. **Copy the request below.** Select the text in the box, copy it, and paste it into the message box in your conversation.

> Help me write a short LinkedIn post announcing my job search. Use the details I give you. Say what role I want, give one example of work I have done, ask my network for one specific kind of help, and include one thing I choose to offer others. If a detail is missing, ask me before drafting. Leave my reasons for job searching private. Give me one draft to review.

3. **Add your details and send.** Under the request, finish these sentences in your own words: “I am looking for ___. One thing I have done is ___. I would welcome ___. I can offer ___. People can reach me by ___.” For a practice run, use this fictional example instead: “I am looking for a Senior Information Technology Technical Project Manager role in the life sciences industry. I coordinated a software rollout with technology and quality teams. I would welcome introductions to life sciences teams hiring for technical project management. I can share relevant openings with other project managers. People can send me a private message.”
4. **Read the draft as someone who knows you.** Is every claim accurate? Would a former colleague know whom to introduce you to? Can you deliver the help you offered? If a sentence sounds unlike you, try “Make the opening more conversational and keep my example exactly as I gave it.”
5. **Choose what to share.** Copy the approved draft into your own LinkedIn post, read it once more, and publish when ready. If you used the fictional example, replace it with your own facts before sharing.

## Use the rest of the collection

6. **Save your preferences once.** Open the [blank job search profile](https://github.com/tafell/Career-Job-Search/blob/main/templates/job-search-profile.md). GitHub is the public bookshelf for these instructions. Select the template text and copy it into a private document on your device. Fill in the parts you know, then paste or upload your copy in your conversation when needed. Keep your completed profile, résumé, and contacts private. Reading the public pages requires no GitHub account or installation.
7. **Choose a task.** Open the [numbered workflow guide](https://github.com/tafell/Career-Job-Search/blob/main/guides/WORKFLOWS.md#job-search-workflow). For example, if you already have a posting, choose **4. Job Posting Review**. Open the instructions for [ChatGPT](https://github.com/tafell/Career-Job-Search/blob/main/chatgpt/skills/job-posting-review/SKILL.md) or [Claude](https://github.com/tafell/Career-Job-Search/blob/main/skills/job-posting-review/SKILL.md). A “skill” is simply the set of instructions on that page.
8. **Use the instructions in your conversation.** Select and copy the numbered instructions, paste them into your chat, then paste the posting and provide your private profile and résumé or relevant experience. Ask “Using these instructions, should I pursue this role? Tell me what fits and what I need to verify.” If the posting link cannot be read, paste the job description itself.
9. **Keep a simple record.** Save the employer, role, posting link, decision, and next action in a document or spreadsheet you already use. You submit your own application. After applying, use **7. Job Connection Outreach** to identify someone who could help and draft a private message.

For email alerts, shared folders, or a tracker you already use, follow [Set up the tools you already use](https://github.com/tafell/Career-Job-Search/blob/main/guides/GETTING-STARTED.md#set-up-the-tools-you-already-use) when you need it.
