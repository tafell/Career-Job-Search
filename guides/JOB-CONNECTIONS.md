# Find Connections for a Job

After submitting your application, use Job Connection Outreach to identify relevant people and prepare a specific request. It distinguishes verified connections from public prospects.

## Start with

1. The full job posting or employer link.
2. A private connection export, relevant connection list, or accessible connection information. A small targeted list is enough to start.
3. Optional relationship notes: how you know a person, when you last spoke, and whether an introduction or referral would be appropriate.
4. Your private profile and source resume when the draft should include candidate achievements.

## Example request

1. "I have applied for this role. Use job-connection-outreach to find connections relevant to this job. Search my attached connection list, verify current roles where possible, and rank a short list. Separate verified connections from public prospects. Recommend an insight request, introduction, or referral for each person and draft the messages. Do not send anything."

## What you receive

1. A recommended first contact with a reason, or an explicit statement that the supplied data contains no verified connection.
2. A numbered contact table with relationship evidence, current role, posting relevance, source dates, and uncertainties.
3. A specific request and draft message for each recommended contact.
4. Proposed next actions and follow-up dates, with no automatic sending or connection requests.

## Limits and privacy

1. Public profile search does not prove that someone is your connection. Name-based search cannot enumerate your full network.
2. Connection exports can be stale. A connection date does not prove a close relationship, and a title does not prove hiring authority.
3. When access is unavailable, provide pasted text or a private export. The workflow does not bypass access controls or sign in for you.
4. Keep connection lists, relationship notes, and message drafts private. Do not upload them to the public repository or a public assistant's knowledge.

## Platform use

1. The workflow lives in [Job Connection Outreach](../chatgpt/skills/job-connection-outreach/SKILL.md). ChatGPT users can follow the project setup guide.
2. Claude users can paste the same numbered instructions into a private conversation or project and supply the same profile and connection data.
3. The shared profile template is unchanged. Supply connection data per task; a permanent connection-source field remains optional and is not required.
